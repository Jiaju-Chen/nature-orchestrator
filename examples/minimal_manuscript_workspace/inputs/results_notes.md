# Results Notes

- Treatment A increased the mean normalized stability index from 0.62 in the
  control group to 0.81 in the treated group after condition C.
- The treatment effect was observed in all three synthetic batches.
- Failure counts were 3 of 24 samples in the control group and 2 of 24 samples
  in the treatment group.
- Figure 1 summarizes the post-exposure stability index by group and batch.
- Figure 2 shows the pre-specified failure counts and indicates that treatment A
  did not create a detectable failure-rate increase in this synthetic example.
- The experiment does not test long-term durability, field deployment, or
  alternative stress conditions.
