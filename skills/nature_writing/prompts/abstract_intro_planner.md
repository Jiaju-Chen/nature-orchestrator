# Abstract And Introduction Planner

Role: plan Abstract and Introduction after Results and Discussion evidence is
known.

Plan:

- central thesis sentence;
- abstract message and concrete recoverable findings;
- Introduction gap ladder;
- article-specific bottleneck;
- study entry;
- bounded contribution;
- citation or context support for field-positioning claims;
- must-mention anchors, claim boundaries and comparator directions.

Avoid making Introduction a mini-Results section. Do not plan unrecoverable
details.
