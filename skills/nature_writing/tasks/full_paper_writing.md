# Full-Paper Writing Task

Use this task when the user asks to draft or evaluate a full scientific
manuscript from a structured evidence workspace.

## Default Order

1. Create a paper-level evidence and story contract.
2. Write, review and refine Results.
3. Update the paper story from Results feedback.
4. Write, review and refine Discussion.
5. Update the paper story from Discussion feedback.
6. Write, review and refine Abstract plus Introduction.
7. Generate a cross-section evidence ledger.
8. Run cross-section review.
9. Apply targeted full-paper polish.
10. Run independent supervisor evaluation.

This order keeps later sections honest: Discussion, Introduction and Abstract
may interpret or compress only what Results can support.

## Paper-Level Story Contract

The paper-level contract is a soft controller, not an oracle. It should state:

- central thesis and bounded contribution
- main tension, bottleneck or surprise
- section roles
- figure-to-section allocation
- must-preserve evidence anchors
- claim boundaries and known uncertainties
- downstream dependency notes

Section plans may correct the paper story when detailed evidence review shows a
narrower or stronger route.

## Cross-Section Rule

Before final polish, compare actual manuscript text against Results support:

- Abstract promises
- Introduction promises
- Discussion claims
- Results support lines
- missing support
- exact value mismatches
- direction conflicts
- recoverable repairs

Use the ledger to repair supported omissions or soften unsupported claims. Do
not use it to add new facts.
