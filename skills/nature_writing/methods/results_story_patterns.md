# Results Story Patterns

Use these as generic section-planning rules, not as facts about the current
paper.

- Organize Results as a chain of findings, not a panel-by-panel catalogue.
- For each major finding, state purpose, observation, comparator or boundary,
  evidence anchor and why the next step follows.
- Give primary results more prose weight than setup and validation.
- Keep controls, nulls, failures and specificity tests close to the claims they
  qualify.
- Preserve recoverable exact values, directions, baselines and comparator
  groups when they change the meaning of the result.
- Distinguish demonstrated findings from projections, model outputs,
  correlations, examples and hypotheses.
- Avoid Discussion-style implications unless they are needed as a local
  transition.
