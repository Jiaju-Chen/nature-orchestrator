# Supervisor Evaluation Method

Use the supervisor only after generation. The supervisor is an independent
evaluator, not a refiner.

## Purpose

The supervisor scores whether the final manuscript is evidence-grounded,
section-appropriate, logically coherent, publication-style writing. It should
also catch reviewer false passes.

## What To Score

Evaluate:

- evidence fidelity and unsupported claim risk
- figure-role understanding and must-mention anchor recall
- Results support for Abstract, Introduction and Discussion promises
- story logic, paragraph function and sentence-to-sentence flow
- section-specific quality
- citation or context grounding for background claims
- calibrated limitations and implications
- venue fit and manuscript readability

## Output

Write structured scores, concrete diagnoses and short examples. Do not rewrite
the manuscript in the supervisor report.
