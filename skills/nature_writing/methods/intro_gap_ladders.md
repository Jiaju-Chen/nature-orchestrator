# Introduction Gap Ladders

Use these as generic Introduction-writing rules.

- Move from field importance to known progress to the article-specific
  bottleneck.
- Make the bottleneck visible before the study entry.
- Use citations or context support for background and prior-work claims.
- Explain why the method or evidence unlocks the contribution.
- Preview the contribution without turning the Introduction into Results.
- Keep the final paragraph specific to this paper.
